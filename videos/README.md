## Format Requirements
- Format: MP4 (H.264 video codec, AAC audio)
- Resolution: 1920x1080 (1080p) recommended
- Duration: 30 seconds - 5 minutes per video

## How to Add Videos
1. Convert your videos to MP4 format
2. Save them in this `videos/` folder with the names listed above
3. Videos will automatically display on all pages with poster images from the `images/` folder

## Notes
- All videos display with `controls` enabled (users can play/pause)
- Poster images are SVG placeholders from `images/classic1.svg`, `classic2.svg`, `classic3.svg`
- Videos are responsive and adapt to screen size
